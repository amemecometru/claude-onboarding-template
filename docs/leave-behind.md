# Claude — One-Page Sheet

Leave this with the team after the onboarding session.

If you are a client reading this: you do not need to understand how Claude
works under the hood. Follow the steps on this sheet. If you are the person
doing the onboarding, the skill and the runbook are your working documents.

## Opening Claude

- **Claude (desktop app)** — the app on your computer. This is where Claude
  can work with folders on your computer: point it at your work folder and
  describe what you need done. (This used to be called Cowork — same
  ability, no separate switch any more.)
- **Claude Code** — the terminal version, for anyone who writes code.
- **Claude on the web** — chat at claude.ai. Good for questions; it cannot
  work with folders on your computer the way the desktop app can.

Sign in with your own work email. Never share one login across the team.
